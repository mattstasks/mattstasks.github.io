## Project Background
This project was developed from the perspective of a consulting team engaged to support a movie app development team with data-driven insights for its streaming platform. The overall project focuses on improving content acquisition, catalogue composition and user content discovery through applied data science.

The team explored three complementary business objectives:

1. Improve user engagement and content discovery through personalised movie recommendations based on content similarity.
2. Maximise return on content licensing spend by predicting movie ROI and identifying higher-potential licensing candidates.
3. Identify movies with characteristics associated with enduring classic status to uncover valuable catalogue content that may be overlooked by acquisition strategies focused mainly on recent commercial performance.
